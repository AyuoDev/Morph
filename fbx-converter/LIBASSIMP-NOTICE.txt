2006 the assimp team, licensed under the 3-clause BSD license. The engine is
consumed as the `taucad/assimp` fork through the `assimp/` git submodule; its

The engine links the following third-party projects, fetched at configure time
by assimp's own CMake build. Each project's license text is retained in its
fetched source tree:

- LightUSD (lighttransport/LightUSD, consumed as the taucad/tinyusdz fork),
  Apache License 2.0 — USD, USDA, USDC, USDZ and MaterialX support. Copyright
  2020-2023 Syoyo Fujita and 2024-present Light Transport Entertainment Inc.
  The third-party code it bundles is listed in its LICENSE.3rdparty.
- engine_web-ifc (ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc), Mozilla Public License 2.0 — IFC
  support. Its source is fetched unmodified apart from a C++20 compatibility
  patch retained beside the engine; obtain it from
  https://github.com/ThatOpen/engine_web-ifc.
- fast_float, licensed under Apache-2.0, MIT, or the Boost Software License.
- tinynurbs (QuimMoya/tinynurbs), 3-clause BSD license.
- glm, MIT license (the Happy Bunny variant).
- CDT (artem-ogre/CDT), Mozilla Public License 2.0.
- spdlog, MIT license.
- stduuid, MIT license.
- lib3mf (3MFConsortium/lib3mf), 2-clause BSD license — 3MF import and export.

The engine also bundles third-party sources under assimp/contrib/, each with
its own license text in place: Open3DGC, USD-Fileformat-plugins, clipper,
draco, earcut-hpp, googletest, meshlab, openddlparser, poly2tri, pugixml, pxr,
rapidjson, stb, unzip, utf8cpp, zip, and zlib.

libassimp is a cleanroom implementation. kovacsv/assimpjs is credited as prior
art for demonstrating that assimp compiles and runs usefully under Emscripten;
no code from it is copied or derived here.
